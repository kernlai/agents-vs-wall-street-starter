## 4. Reconciling the revenue basis (important — the team's target is "worldwide net sales and revenues")

Zacks and most retail aggregators report Deere "revenue" as **equipment-operations net sales**, which
*excludes* finance & interest income and other income. Our forecast target is **worldwide net sales and
revenues** (the headline line in Deere's press release). These differ by ~$1.6 bn per quarter.

Proof the Zacks series is net-sales basis (REPORTED FACTS from corpus + arithmetic):

| Q3 FY2025 (actual) | $12.018 bn | $10.357 bn | $1.661 bn |
| Q4 FY2025 (actual) | $12.394 bn | ~$10.58 bn (derived) | ~$1.81 bn |
| Q1 FY2026 (actual) | $9.611 bn | $8.001 bn (derived) | $1.610 bn |
| Q2 FY2026 (actual) | $13.369 bn | $11.778 bn | $1.591 bn |

Sources: `challenge/offline-data/deere/filings/2025-08-15__de-us-20250815-q3-8k__143410.md`,
`.../2025-11-26__de-us-20251126-q4-8k__361233.md`, `.../2026-02-19__de-us-20260219-q1-8k__603009.md`,
`.../2026-05-21__de-us-20260521-q2-8k__1042167.md`.

Cross-check: Zacks FY2026 revenue $41.51 bn at "+6.66%" implies FY2025 base of **$38.92 bn** = FY2025
*net sales*, not the $45.68 bn FY2025 worldwide net sales & revenues. Confirmed net-sales basis.

**MY INFERENCE — consensus worldwide net sales & revenues, Q3 FY2026:**

$10.87 bn (consensus net sales) + $1.59–1.66 bn (recent FS/other gap) = **$12.46 – $12.53 bn**.
Point estimate **≈ $12.5 bn**, i.e. **+4.0% YoY** vs $12.018 bn.

If the team quotes a "consensus revenue" number, it must say which basis. Quoting $10.87 bn against a
reported $12.5 bn would look like a 15% beat that never happened.
