CONTROLLED SPEC -- isolating the COST channel from the PRICING/DEMAND channel
==============================================================================
The bivariate result above is confounded: steel prices are procyclical with ag
equipment demand, so a steel spike arrives together with a demand boom that lets
Deere raise list prices. The dependent variable below is the log cost-of-sales
ratio; the controls are Deere's own output-price proxy (PPI ag machinery) and a
volume/absorption proxy (net sales). Steel's coefficient is then the cost channel.
